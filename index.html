Three ingredients and a lot of waiting.

Hazelpine started as one oven in a rented kitchen and a sourdough starter that is older than the business. We still bake the same way: flour, water, salt and time.

We buy grain from two farms in the valley and mill most of it within the week. When a batch is gone, it's gone, and we start the next one that evening.
